Track insurance claim denials as paperwork: deadlines, documents, calls, and appeal levels. Everything stays in this browser.

Education and organization only. This tool helps you organize insurance paperwork and deadlines. It is not medical advice, not legal advice, and does not decide what care you should receive. Deadlines shown are estimates based on the window you enter — always confirm deadlines with your plan documents, denial letter, or your state's insurance regulator.
